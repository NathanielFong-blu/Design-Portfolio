PROJECT 05

DVD Case Making
David Bowie's Space Oddity

GRAPHIC DESIGN | PROTOTYPING

Project Aim Research Prototyping Final Result

PROJECT AIM

A project dedicated to creating an album cover for David Bowie and some of his famous songs. I chose to go with Space Oddity as it was one of my favourite music and I was deeply inspired by Chris Hadfield’s music video.






THEMATIC RESEARCH

I chose to go with an old-schooled 70s comic style as the design motif. Since David Bowie’s vocals reminded me of the narrator’s voice in old comic cartoons, and this song always illustrates a story in my mind.
I took many inspiration from old-school comics, pre cold-war era concept art about space, to modern fiction and media, with an emphasis on space disaster, matching the song's story.







CONCEPT BUILDING

I made some concept draft of how the overall theme, artstylae should look like. I then experimented with different positioning of the rocket, comic panels and color palette.




Feedback:
- brighter color fits the dramatic theme of the song
- comic panels make the cover to dense and complicated
- better font selection





FINAL RESULTS

Based on feedbacks:
- more saturated variaty in color
- use shorter font to fit in the corner and give more space to main contents
- remove comic panels to give more space
I then created different panels to depict the story within Space Oddity. I then created DVD case with the printed materials.
