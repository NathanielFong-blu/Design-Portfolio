PROJECT AIM

Problem Statement: Many market leaders portray cage-free eggs as humane to attract more conciouse consumers

Through market research, analysing current cultural climate, user reasearch and prototyping, this project aims to create a barnd that re-frame consumer understanding surrounding the harsh treatment of hens in cage-free egg products and as a result remove them from our shelves.





CONSUMER RESEARCH & COMPETITOR ANALYSIS

We conducted our research through small sample sized survey, online forums and existing survey results:

Under current cumsumer climate, while majority of them, especially younger demographics, would support a more humane way to consume eggs, many of them are unaware of cage-free eggs' cruelty to hens.

As for market leaders in cage-free egss (Coles, Sunny Queen Farms), many tend to hide such cruelty through the use of bright optimistic colors, family-friendly illustrations and depiction of healthy hens.


Thus we decided through juxtaposing market leaders' way of portraying cage-free eggs as humane, we could create a brand that encourage consumer to become more aware of the issue, by using a more optmisitic energy and non-market standard packaging to attract customers and highlight the hidden cruelty behind cage-free eggs.



CONSUMER'S PERSONALITY MATRIX

Based on our research, we designed a Personality matrix for the typical consumer we expect to be interested. We also create a brand identity we aim to achieve to reach our customers and initial goal.











PROTYPING & USER RESEARCH

Based on our identity, we experimented with different logo branding, packaging design, colour and inforgraphics. We then conducted a small sample user research, where we received many feedbacks.








Through the user research, we learnt:
- packaging design seems plain and uninteresting
- some imagerys' style do not fit
- packaging is very interesting, grabbed many's attention
- packaging is intuitive and easy to use


Reflection and iterations:
- colors should be brighter and more saturated stand out
- more cartoonish imagery are needed to stand out
- inforgraphics should be more legible





FINAL RESULT

We iterated and created a final design and conducted another user research:
- stands out among other odinary egg product on the shelf
- inforgraphics are more legible due to removal of strips
- packaging is intersting -> further investigation by consumers -> higher chance to read our inforgraphics.

Overall, we are very satisfy with our result, as we are able to improve upon user feedbacks and create an engaging product that achieved our original goals.
